    It starts empty on purpose. Whatever you write is the first thing anyone reads here.

    How this wall works

    • A person reads it first. Your post waits until it has been approved. You can see your own while it waits. Nobody else can.
    • No name, no account. Nothing you post is tied to who you are.
    • No downvotes. You can mark a post as helpful, and that is the only number on this page. There is nothing here to lose.
    • Take it down any time. Your own posts, whenever you want.
    • Report anything cruel. One report pulls a post off the wall straight away, before anyone reviews it.

    Do not post anything that identifies you or anyone else. No full names, no schools, no handles, no phone numbers. This page checks for those and will ask you twice, but it cannot catch everything.
