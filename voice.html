    Act three · your turn

    Say what you need to say.

    Every story here is anonymous. Every reply is a hand on a shoulder.

    House rules Read all five →

    Leave names out of it. Yours, your school's, and the name of anyone your story is about. You can tell the whole truth without handing over anyone's identity.

    This wall lifts people up. Nothing cruel, nothing that outs someone, nothing that tells another person how to hurt themselves.

    Right now, posts stay on your device only. Nothing is uploaded, so nobody else online can read what you write yet. That also means anyone who uses this same phone or laptop can, so take a post down when you are done. A shared, moderated wall is coming, and it will open with these same rules.

    Posted anonymously, no names, no accounts

    Be gentle with each other. This wall lifts people up. It never tears them down.
