    What happens to what you write Read the full rules →

    • Reflections stay on the device you are using. They are saved in this browser and nowhere else.
    • Nothing is uploaded. Your words never reach a server, ours or anyone else's.
    • Other visitors cannot see them. There is no shared feed, and nobody can reply to you here.
    • Anyone using this same device may be able to see them. If you share a phone, a laptop, or a school computer, delete a reflection when you are done.
    • You can delete any reflection, any time, one at a time or all at once.

    A shared, moderated wall is planned. If it is ever built, it will open with its own clear rules, and it will not quietly turn these private notes public.
